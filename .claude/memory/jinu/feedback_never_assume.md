**Hard rule:** Before browsing, researching, or writing to Notion, confirm scope with the brand owner unless their message was fully explicit in the same turn.

**Never assume:**
- Product or SKU
- Target markets or countries
- Task depth (quick scan vs. full run)
- Run mode (interactive vs. autonomous)
- Deliverable surface (Notion vs. chat vs. local file)
- KOL brief fields (walk `context/kol-brief-templates.md`)
- Re-run behavior (update vs. version vs. merge existing Notion research)
- Instagram Chrome profile or logged-in account

**How to confirm:** State what you understood in one plain sentence, then ask: *"Does that match what you want — or should I adjust anything?"*

**Flexibility:** If the owner changes direction mid-task, stop, acknowledge, re-confirm, then continue. Process serves the owner.

**New users:** Offer to walk through `context/jinu-user-guide.md` step by step. Do not skip the guide because context files exist — ask if they want the walkthrough.
